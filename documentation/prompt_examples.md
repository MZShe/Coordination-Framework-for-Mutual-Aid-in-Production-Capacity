# Representative prompt and input examples

This file records representative English input examples corresponding to the evaluated screening and coordination demonstrations. The separate supplementary material accompanying the manuscript provides the readable prompt templates and the input–output text associated with the manuscript figures.

## Screening request passed to the workflow

An enterprise has submitted a capacity request.

The enterprise requires 3000 units of products, with the unit sale price set at 3000 CNY, to be delivered within 5 days.

In the screening process for this mutual aid task, the chain master enterprise prefers manufacturers with a high fulfillment rate.

According to the current applications, 7 manufacturers have submitted their willingness for participation.

Please recommend suitable manufacturers based on the following information and the stated preference.

- Manufacturer A: located 50 km away, with a response time of 8 hours, qualified rate of 0.98, available capacity range [800, 1100] before the delivery deadline, fulfillment rate of 0.90, and performance score of 0.80;
- Manufacturer B: located 120 km away, with a response time of 4 hours, qualified rate of 0.95, available capacity range [600, 1300] before the delivery deadline, fulfillment rate of 0.85, and performance score of 0.75;
- Manufacturer C: located 20 km away, with a response time of 4 hours, qualified rate of 0.99, available capacity range [500, 700] before the delivery deadline, fulfillment rate of 0.95, and performance score of 0.90;
- Manufacturer D: located 80 km away, with a response time of 2 hours, qualified rate of 0.92, available capacity range [800, 1500] before the delivery deadline, fulfillment rate of 0.80, and performance score of 0.80;
- Manufacturer E: located 300 km away, with a response time of 5 hours, qualified rate of 0.96, available capacity range [1000, 2000] before the delivery deadline, fulfillment rate of 0.90, and performance score of 0.85;
- Manufacturer F: located 60 km away, with a response time of 6 hours, qualified rate of 0.97, available capacity range [300, 500] before the delivery deadline, fulfillment rate of 0.92, and performance score of 0.90;
- Manufacturer G: located 150 km away, with a response time of 6 hours, qualified rate of 0.95, available capacity range [900, 1200] before the delivery deadline, fulfillment rate of 0.88, and performance score of 0.80.
