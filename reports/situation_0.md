# Situation 0 — No additional preference

A Decision Analysis Report

## Execution Recommendation

The selected set {D, C, A, B} meets the screening criteria of having at least two suppliers with a combined capacity midpoint reaching 115% of the demand. The total capacity midpoint is 3650.0, which exceeds the required 3000 units.

## Capacity Risk Analysis

The lower bound of the combined capacity is 2700.0, which does not cover the full demand. However, the upper bound reaches 4600.0, providing a buffer of 1600.0 units. This allows for flexibility in production planning, although demand fulfillment depends on the capacity actually available.

## Key Supplier Evaluation

Manufacturer D has the highest TOPSIS score and the shortest response time, but its fulfillment rate is the lowest among the candidates.

Manufacturer C offers the shortest distance and highest quality rate, and shares the highest performance score with F, but has comparatively limited capacity.

Manufacturer A combines a high quality rate with a relatively high fulfillment rate, but has the longest response time among the selected manufacturers.

Manufacturer B contributes a capacity range of 600–1300 units, but has the lowest performance score and is the farthest away among the selected manufacturers.

## Alternative Strategy

Manufacturer F is the highest-ranked unselected candidate, with a joint-highest performance score and the second-highest fulfillment rate. However, its available capacity is only 300–500 units, so any proposed substitution requires reassessing the resulting set against the screening criteria and task demand.
