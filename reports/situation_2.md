# Situation 2 — Geographical proximity and performance emphasized

A Decision Analysis Report

## Execution Recommendation

The recommended mutual aid set is {C, A, D, F, B}. Its combined capacity midpoint is 4050 units, and the aggregate lower bound of 3000 units matches the task demand.

## Capacity Risk Analysis

The selected suppliers’ combined lower bound capacity is exactly 3000 units, matching the demand. The upper bound is 5100 units, providing a buffer of 2100 units. This range offers flexibility in production planning, but no capacity margin at the lower bound.

## Key Supplier Evaluation

Manufacturer C offers the highest TOPSIS score with strong quality and fulfillment rates, though its available capacity is limited.

Manufacturer A has a high quality rate but a longer response time.

Manufacturer D excels in response speed but has a lower fulfillment rate.

Manufacturer F combines high quality and performance with limited available capacity.

Manufacturer B has a relatively broad capacity range but the lowest performance score.

## Alternative Strategy

Manufacturer G, the top-ranked non-selected candidate, offers a capacity range of 900–1200 units, but is farther away and has a lower TOPSIS score than the selected manufacturers. It could be considered as an alternative if further evaluation confirms its viability.
