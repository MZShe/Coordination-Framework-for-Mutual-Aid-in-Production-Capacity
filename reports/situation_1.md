# Situation 1 — Fulfillment rate prioritized

A Decision Analysis Report

## Execution Recommendation

The recommended mutual aid set {D, C, A, B} is advised for execution. It satisfies the criterion of including at least 2 manufacturers, and the sum of the individual capacity midpoints is 3650.0, which exceeds 115% of the required demand.

## Capacity Risk Analysis

The selected set has a total capacity range from 2700 to 4600, with the lower bound being 2700, which is less than the required quantity. This indicates a potential risk of insufficient capacity if all selected manufacturers can only provide capacities near their lower bounds. However, the upper bound provides sufficient capacity to satisfy the task demand.

## Key Supplier Evaluation

Manufacturer D provides a relatively large capacity range and a fast response time, making it a strong candidate for the task.

Manufacturer C combines the shortest distance with high quality and fulfillment performance.

Manufacturer A has a good capacity match but a slower response time.

Manufacturer B provides a moderate capacity with acceptable quality and performance.

## Alternative Strategy

Since the set is not full (only 4 out of 7 suppliers are selected), a contingency plan is available. If any of the selected suppliers fail, Manufacturer F, which is ranked first by TOPSIS among the remaining suppliers, can serve as a backup, ensuring some level of redundancy and reducing the overall risk.

The report concludes with a recommendation to proceed with the selected collaborative set while keeping an eye on potential risks and having a backup plan in place.
